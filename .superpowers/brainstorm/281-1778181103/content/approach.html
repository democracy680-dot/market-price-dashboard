How should the Quarterly Results tab work for you?

Your goal: find stocks that announced strong results today and are likely to keep moving up for weeks. Which approach fits your workflow?

A

Smart Score Card — One number to cut through the noise

Each stock gets a single "Post-Result Strength Score" (0–100) combining technical setup, momentum, and fundamentals. You sort by score and focus only on the top-ranked stocks. Clean and fast — one column tells you where to look.

Best for: Quick daily scan. You want to open the tab and immediately know which 3–5 stocks deserve deeper investigation.

B

Full Signal Panel — See everything, decide yourself

Add individual signal columns: Trend Status (200-DMA), Minervini Score (0–8), RS Rank (relative strength vs Nifty), Volume Surge (today's vol vs avg). All data visible — no black box scoring.

Best for: You trust your own judgement. You want the raw signals so you can make the final call.

C

Score + Signals — Best of both

A composite score column plus the key individual signals. The score ranks them for you, but the signal breakdown shows why a stock scored high — so you can validate or override. Slightly more columns, but maximum information density.

Best for: You want speed AND transparency. Score gets you to the right stocks fast; signals confirm the thesis.
