What should the Score primarily measure?

This determines what the "Post-Result Strength Score" rewards. Think about how you actually pick stocks after results season.

A

Pre-result technical setup

Reward stocks that were already strong before results day — uptrend, above 200-DMA, strong relative strength vs Nifty, Minervini template passing. The idea: great results in a stock already in stage-2 = continuation for weeks.

📐 Signals: Minervini score (0–8), RS Rank, 200-DMA status, ADX trend strength
B

Market reaction on results day

Reward stocks where the market reacted strongly to the results — big positive return AND unusually high volume. The idea: institutions are entering, and price + volume confirmation signals sustained buying.

📊 Signals: Announcement-day return %, Volume surge (today vs 20-day avg), Gap-up magnitude
C

Both — setup quality × reaction strength (Recommended)

The highest-conviction stocks have both: a clean technical base before results AND strong market reaction on the day. A stock in stage-2 uptrend that also gaps up on high volume is a rare, high-quality setup.

🎯 Score = (Setup Quality 50%) + (Results Reaction 30%) + (Fundamentals 20%)
